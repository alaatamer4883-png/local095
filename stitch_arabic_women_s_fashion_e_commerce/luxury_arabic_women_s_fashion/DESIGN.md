## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, editorial-first luxury fashion commerce foundation engineered natively for RTL (Right-to-Left) reading rhythms and multi-script harmony. The aesthetic pairs the restraint of contemporary Parisian editorial layouts with the tactile warmth and architectural balance of Middle Eastern couture.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes serenity, discernment, and prestige. Rather than screaming for attention with aggressive commercial badges, it uses expansive cream negative space, precise proportion, and disciplined typographic hierarchy. The customer experiences a curated salon environment that feels bespoke, secure, and respectful of cultural elegance.

### Design Movement
- **Minimalist Editorial Luxury:** Spacious canvases, quiet backgrounds, and content-first gallery framing.
- **Architectural Structure:** Strict underlying grid discipline offset by humanistic Arabic typography.
- **Subtle Tactility:** Restrained warm champagne accents against soft alabaster layers rather than high-contrast stark whites.

## Colors

The palette relies on light, warm surfaces, quiet foundational borders, and deep charcoal text to deliver effortless legibility and timeless luxury.

- **Primary Accent (`#B38938`):** Muted champagne gold. Reserved strictly for primary calls to action, active navigation indicators, key interactive cues, and scarcity alerts.
- **Secondary Surface (`#EAE5DF`):** Soft sand beige. Used for card backgrounds, tertiary containers, and tactile surface separation.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D8A499`):** Subtle desert blush. Used sparingly for curated seasonal tags, whisper highlights, and delicate editorial accents.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep charcoal black. Replaces pure black to provide softer contrast against light cream backgrounds while maintaining WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Base Background (`#FAF9F6`):** Warm alabaster cream. Provides an organic, textile-like undertone across all primary canvas spaces.
- **Dividers & Outlines (`#E5DFD7`):** Fine structural borders separating sections and subtle input boundaries without optical weight.

## Typography

Typographic scale is balanced for Arabic and Latin text rendering simultaneously. Arabic script requires slightly greater vertical line height than Latin to accommodate natural ascenders and descenders without clipping or dense visual tension.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Light to medium weights (`300` and `400`) deliver an airy, high-fashion cadence.
- **Body & Product Metadata:** Standard regular weight (`400`) across all body copy ensures effortless reading across long descriptions and garment specifications.
- **Micro-labels & Badging:** Medium and Semi-bold (`500` and `600`) with modest optical tracking for uppercase Latin and crisp Arabic clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an editorial fluid grid with generous horizontal margins that frame product imagery like art pieces in a gallery.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, `margin` of `3rem` (48px), and `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px). Hero features and product lookbooks span asymmetrical spans (e.g., 7 columns for imagery, 5 columns for typography and purchasing controls).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins. E-commerce listings adapt to 2 or 3 items per row.
- **Mobile (0px - 767px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile` of `0.75rem` (12px), and `margin-mobile` of `1rem` (16px). Product listings default to a refined 2-column or high-impact full-bleed 1-column layout.
- **RTL Fluidity:** In full RTL mode, structural margins anchor primary scanning starting from the top-right canvas edge down toward the lower left.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm tonal shifts and soft, ambient diffused shadows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Base `#FAF9F6` background. No elevation or border.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Containers):** Elevated with `#F4F0EB` or bordered with a subtle `1px` `#E5DFD7` outline. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky Header, Dropdowns):** Background pure white or `#FAF9F6`. Ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs, Quick View, Cart Drawers):** Background `#FAF9F6` framed with a soft inner hairline border. Ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts roundedness level `2` (8px standard corner radius). This creates a polished, organic curve that softens rigid retail boundaries while maintaining structural luxury.

- **Standard Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Sub-Containers & Nested Elements:** `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px) for interior product highlights and trust modules.
- **Drawers & Modals:** `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px) along leading entry borders (top edges on mobile sheets, corner radii on dialogs).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid champagne gold (`#B38938`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. On hover, transitions to a deeper warm gold (`#9D762D`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Ghost structure with `1px` solid `#1A1A1A` outline, transparent background, and `#1A1A1A` text.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Understated text with an animated bottom border offset by `4px`, directional arrows flip seamlessly in RTL (`←` becomes `→`).

### Product Cards
- Warm sand surface (`#F4F0EB`) or unbordered image frame on `#FAF9F6`.
- Aspect ratio of `3:4` or `4:5` for editorial proportion.
- Scarcity indicators (e.g., "قطعة واحدة متبقية" / "Only 1 Left") positioned at top-left in RTL layouts in a subtle pill badge with blush tint (`#D8A499` at 15% opacity, `#1A1A1A` text).

### Chips & Tags
- `0.5rem` rounded corners, muted `#EAE5DF` fill, `#1A1A1A` label text.
- Active filter chip transitions to `#1A1A1A` background with `#FAF9F6` text.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Neutral border (`1px` `#E5DFD7`) on a clean `#FAF9F6` base.
- Focused state transitions border to `#B38938` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(179, 137, 56, 0.15)`).
- Input labels and placeholder text align right in RTL mode with matching baseline offsets.

### Trust Badges & Guarantees
- Compact horizontal lockups combining subtle vector line icons with dual-line microcopy ("أصالة مضمونة" / "100% Authentic Guarantee").
- Enclosed in a soft sand card (`#F4F0EB`) with `0.5rem` radius and `1px` border in `#E5DFD7`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: `0.25rem` radius, `1.5px` border in `#1A1A1A`. Checked state displays `#B38938` fill with a crisp white check icon.
- Radios: Circular, dual-ring structure with `#B38938` focal center dot on active selection.
